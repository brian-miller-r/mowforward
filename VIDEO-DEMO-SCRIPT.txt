MISSION MOW DEMO SCRIPT

Aim for about 2 minutes and 30 seconds. Keep the final video under 3 minutes.

Before recording, open a fresh private browser window so the site has clean
sample data. Use only fake information in the forms. Turn on Do Not Disturb,
make the browser window easy to read, and do one practice run.

0:00 to 0:30 - Home page

Henry says:

Hi, I’m Henry Miller. I’m in seventh grade at Prince Sam Middle School. I got
this idea when my dad offered to help mow our friend’s lawn while her husband
was deployed and she was caring for a toddler. My app is called Mission Mow.
It connects military families who need lawn help with local lawn-care
businesses that can donate one extra stop on a route they already serve.

I built it for military families in Virginia’s 2nd District, where many
families manage home life while someone is away serving.

Show the home page and the How Mission Mow Works section.

0:30 to 0:45 - Map and impact

Henry says:

The map shows a sample Hampton Roads network. Blue markers represent lawn
businesses and green markers represent participating home areas. The Impact
Board starts with sample activity and updates after a mow is completed in the
app.

Show the map and the Community Impact Board.

0:45 to 1:20 - Family flow

Henry says:

On the family side, someone creates a profile and posts a lawn request. The
request only needs the details that help a crew decide whether it fits their
route, like ZIP code, lawn size, and preferred days. It begins as Open.

Go to Families. Create a fake profile called Demo Family. Use a fake email.
Create a request in ZIP code 23456, choose a lawn size and preferred days, and
post the request.

1:20 to 1:55 - Business flow

Henry says:

On the business side, a lawn-care company joins with the ZIP codes it already
serves. It sees open requests nearby, claims one that fits its route, and
marks it complete when the work is done. This makes donating time practical
for a small local business.

Go to Businesses. Join as Demo Lawn Co. with a fake email and ZIP code 23456.
Claim the new request, then mark the mow complete.

1:55 to 2:10 - Yard sign and result

Henry says:

A completed mow can include a yard sign that credits the business and helps
another family or crew discover Mission Mow. When the mow is complete, the
Impact Board increases automatically.

Show the business yard-sign section, then return home and show the updated
Impact Board.

2:10 to 2:35 - Technology

Henry says:

I built Mission Mow as a web app with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. For this
version, data is stored in the browser so I could focus on the family and
business matching flow.

The hardest part was making the overall layout of the site and just
configuring all of the controls and stuff. I came up with an idea by using
ChatGPT to help me figure out ideas and build the site.

2:35 to 2:50 - Closing

Henry says:

If I built a 2.0, I would add a real backend, military-family verification,
and notifications. I learned that a useful app needs to make things easier
for both the people asking for help and the businesses giving it. Thank you.
