Mission Mow
Demo Script

Keep the recording under 3 minutes. Henry narrates while the site is operated on the right.

0:00-0:30 · Home

Introduce Mission Mow

“Hi, I’m Henry Miller. I’m in seventh grade at Prince Sam Middle School. I got this idea when my dad offered to help mow our friend’s lawn while her husband was deployed and she was caring for a toddler. My app is called Mission Mow. It connects military families who need lawn help with local lawn-care businesses that can donate one extra stop on a route they already serve.”

Show: Home page and How Mission Mow Works.

0:30-0:45 · Map

Show the local idea

“The map shows a sample Hampton Roads network. Blue markers represent lawn businesses and green markers represent participating home areas. The Impact Board updates after a mow is completed.”

Show: Coverage map and Community Impact Board.

0:45-1:20 · Families

Post a request

“On the family side, someone creates a profile and posts a lawn request. It includes the basic route details a crew needs, like ZIP code, lawn size, and preferred days. It begins as Open.”

Show: Create a fake family profile, then post a request in ZIP 23456.

1:20-1:55 · Businesses

Claim and complete it

“A lawn-care company joins with the ZIP codes it already serves. It sees open requests nearby, claims one that fits its route, and marks it complete when the work is done.”

Show: Join as a fake business with ZIP 23456, claim the request, and mark it complete.

1:55-2:10 · Impact

Show the result

“A completed mow can include a yard sign that credits the business and helps another family or crew discover Mission Mow. The Impact Board increases automatically.”

Show: Yard-sign partner benefit, then the updated Impact Board.

2:10-2:50 · Close

How it was built

“I built Mission Mow with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. The hardest part was making the overall layout of the site and just configuring all of the controls and stuff. I came up with an idea by using ChatGPT to help me figure out ideas and build the site. For a 2.0, I would add a real backend, military-family verification, and notifications. Thank you.”
