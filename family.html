How it works for you

1. Create your profile (this page).

2. Post your lawn with your ZIP code — only your ZIP is ever shown publicly.

3. A local business claims your request. You’ll see exactly who’s coming.

4. Your lawn gets mowed. Free.

For this demo, do not enter an address, gate code, or other private property information.
