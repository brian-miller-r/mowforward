While they serve, we mow.

MowForward connects local lawn care businesses with military families while a spouse is deployed — so a crew already working in the neighborhood can stop by and donate a mow.

How it works

One extra stop on a route a crew is already driving. That's the whole idea.

1

Family posts a request

A military spouse signs up, shares their ZIP and lawn details, and posts a request. No awkward asking.

2

Business claims it nearby

A local lawn company sees open requests in ZIP codes it already serves and claims one in a single click.

3

The lawn gets mowed

The crew stops by on their regular route. The donation lands on the public Impact Board for the whole community to see.

“The people in my district—we are regular, everyday Americans. We just put service to country first.”

Why start in Virginia's 2nd District

Virginia Beach is home to one of the largest military communities in America — Naval Air Station Oceana, Joint Expeditionary Base Little Creek–Fort Story, and Dam Neck are all right here. When deployments pull a spouse away for months, the lawn doesn't wait — but neither do the dozens of lawn care crews already driving these streets every week. MowForward simply introduces them.

For lawn care businesses
