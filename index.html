How Mission Mow works

A simple match with real local impact.

01

A family posts a need

Only ZIP code, lawn size, and availability appear before a business chooses to help.

02

A local business claims it

Requests are matched to ZIP codes that a crew already serves, keeping donation time practical.

03

The community sees the impact

When a mow is complete, the Community Impact Board recognizes the effort.
