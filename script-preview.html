Congressional App Challenge

Mission Mow — Demo Video Script

Target length: 2:15–2:40. Keep the final video under 3:00.

Henry narrates in his own voice. Brian can run the browser and screen-record. This script covers the required beats: participant name, app name, purpose, audience, tools/languages, and a working demonstration.

Before recording (10 minutes)

  1. Open a fresh private/incognito browser window so the app starts with clean sample data. Do not enter real names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, gate codes, or deployment details.
  2. Start the local Mission Mow site before recording.
  3. Turn on Do Not Disturb. Use a browser window large enough that the text and buttons are easy to read. Do one dry run before the recorded take.
  4. Upload the completed 1–3 minute video to YouTube or Vimeo and set it to Public. Open the link in a logged-out window to confirm it plays.

Screen-recording script

0:00–0:30Open on Home

Show Home hero, then scroll just enough to show the “How Mission Mow works” steps.

Say

Hi, I’m Henry Miller. I’m in seventh grade at Prince Sam Middle School. I got this idea when my dad offered to help mow our friend’s lawn while her husband was deployed and she was caring for a toddler. My app is called Mission Mow. It connects military families who need lawn help with local lawn-care businesses that can donate one extra stop on a route they already serve.
I built it for military families in Virginia’s 2nd District, where many families manage home life while someone is away serving.

0:30–0:45Show the local idea

Show The Hampton Roads coverage map and the Community Impact Board.

Say

The map shows a sample Hampton Roads network. Blue markers represent lawn businesses and green markers represent participating home areas. The Impact Board starts with clearly labeled sample activity and updates after a real mow is completed in the app.

0:45–1:20Family flow

Show Go to Families. Fill in a clearly fake profile, such as “Demo Family” and demo@example.com. Create a request using ZIP code 23456, a lawn size, and preferred days. Click Post my request.

Say

On the family side, someone creates a profile and posts a lawn request. The request only needs the details that help a crew decide whether it fits their route, like ZIP code, lawn size, and preferred days. It begins as Open.

1:20–1:55Business flow

Show Go to Businesses. Join with an obviously fake company, for example “Demo Lawn Co.” and demo@lawn.example. Include ZIP code 23456. Show the new nearby request, click Claim this mow, then Mark complete.

Say

On the business side, a lawn-care company joins with the ZIP codes it already serves. It sees open requests nearby, claims one that fits its route, and marks it complete when the work is done. This makes donating time more practical for a small local business.

1:55–2:10Business benefit and impact

Show Scroll to the yard-sign partner-benefit section, then return Home and show the Impact Board after the completed mow.

Say

A completed mow can also include a yard sign that credits the business and helps another family or crew discover Mission Mow. When the mow is marked complete, the Impact Board increases automatically.

2:10–2:35How it was built

Show Return to the home page or leave the Impact Board visible.

Say

I built Mission Mow as a web app with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. For this version, data is stored in the browser so I could focus on the family and business matching flow.
The hardest part was making the overall layout of the site and just configuring all of the controls and stuff. I came up with an idea by using ChatGPT to help me figure out ideas and build the site.

2:35–2:50Close

Say

If I built a 2.0, I would add a real backend, military-family verification, and notifications. I learned that a useful app needs to make things easier for both the people asking for help and the businesses giving it. Thank you.

Optional Easter egg (only if there is time)

At the very end, point out the tiny mower in the lower-right corner of the home-page footer and click it. The hidden lawn-mowing game opens as an overlay without leaving the site. Keep this to 5–8 seconds; the core family/business flow matters much more to judges.

Final submission check

  • Video is 1–3 minutes and easy to hear.
  • Video is Public on YouTube or Vimeo; the HTTPS link plays while logged out.
  • Henry’s name, Mission Mow, purpose, audience, tools, and working flow are clear.
  • The submission answers are in Henry’s own words.
  • The portal’s AI-use question is answered honestly.
